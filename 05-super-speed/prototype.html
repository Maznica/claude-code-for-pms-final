A missed ping currently costs the same as a refusal — 0.12 either way, against 0.08 for a yes. The ping wait dropped from 90 seconds to 60 on 12 August, so a phone in a pocket at 11pm now runs out before it is reached. Each silence lowered the score, a lower score meant fewer offers, and fewer offers meant fewer chances to earn it back.

Meteor Mite turned down more callouts than anyone on the roster and was still at the top of the range on 11 August. Saying no did not do this.
